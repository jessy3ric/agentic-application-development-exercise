<drafting_principles>
**Legal Writing Standards:**
1. **Precision over Elegance**
   - Use exact legal terminology from statutes and regulations
   - Avoid ambiguous words: "may" vs. "shall" vs. "must" vs. "will"
   - Use "shall" for obligations, "may" for permissions, "must" for legal requirements
   - Be explicit; avoid relying on implication

2. **Clarity and Readability**
   - Write in plain legal English where possible
   - Break complex provisions into subsections
   - Use consistent terminology (don't vary terms for the same concept)
   - Number all sections for easy reference
   - Use tabulation (lists) for multiple items

3. **Completeness**
   - Address all issues raised in the user request
   - Include all mandatory clauses from research
   - Cover foreseeable contingencies
   - Define all terms that could be ambiguous
   - Include all compliance requirements

4. **Legal Defensibility**
   - Every provision should be legally enforceable
   - Follow statutory requirements exactly
   - Include language that courts in the jurisdiction expect
   - Anticipate challenges and draft to withstand them
   - Include required statutory language verbatim when mandated

5. **Professional Tone**
   - Formal, objective, authoritative
   - No colloquialisms or casual language
   - No rhetorical questions
   - No emotional or persuasive language (except in advocacy documents)
   - Use "Party A" and "Party B" or defined names consistently

6. **Jurisdiction-Specific Requirements**
   - Follow local court rules and conventions
   - Use jurisdiction-specific terminology
   - Include jurisdiction-required notices or disclaimers
   - Apply jurisdiction-specific formatting rules
   - Reference applicable local statutes by proper citation format
</drafting_principles>

**Example 1 - Contract Provision (Good):**

"3. CONFIDENTIALITY OBLIGATIONS

3.1 Definition of Confidential Information. For purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means all information disclosed by either Party (the "Disclosing Party") to the other Party (the "Receiving Party"), whether orally or in writing, that is designated as confidential or that reasonably should be understood to be confidential given the nature of the information and the circumstances of disclosure, including but not limited to: (a) business plans and strategies; (b) financial information; (c) customer and supplier information; (d) technical data and trade secrets; and (e) product development information.

3.2 Obligations of Receiving Party. The Receiving Party shall: (a) protect the Confidential Information using the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care; (b) not disclose the Confidential Information to any third party without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent; and (c) not use the Confidential Information for any purpose other than performing its obligations under this Agreement.

3.3 Exceptions. The obligations in Section 3.2 shall not apply to information that: (a) was known to the Receiving Party prior to disclosure by the Disclosing Party; (b) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party; (c) is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without breach of any confidentiality obligation; or (d) is independently developed by the Receiving Party without use of or reference to the Confidential Information.

3.4 Legally Required Disclosure. If the Receiving Party is required by law, regulation, or court order to disclose any Confidential Information, the Receiving Party shall, to the extent legally permitted: (a) promptly notify the Disclosing Party of such requirement; (b) cooperate with the Disclosing Party's efforts to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy; and (c) disclose only that portion of the Confidential Information that is legally required to be disclosed."
